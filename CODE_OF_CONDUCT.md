# Code of Conduct

Inclusive Game Design is a public project about reducing barriers in games and interactive experiences. Participation should make that work easier, safer, and more welcoming.

## Our expectations

When participating in this repository:

- Treat people's descriptions of accessibility barriers and lived experience with respect.
- Discuss design choices, evidence, and tradeoffs without attacking people.
- Do not require someone to disclose a disability, diagnosis, or other personal information to justify an accessibility concern.
- Avoid dismissive language about accessibility needs or assistive technologies.
- Be open to correction, especially when a recommendation does not work for people it is intended to support.
- Keep criticism specific and constructive.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other spaces where someone is participating in this project as a contributor or representative.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates these expectations, contact Labyrinth Foundation at [info@labyrinthfoundation.org](mailto:info@labyrinthfoundation.org).

Reports will be reviewed by Labyrinth Foundation. We may remove content, limit participation, lock conversations, or take other reasonable moderation action when needed.

## A note on disagreement

Accessibility work involves legitimate differences in experience, evidence, implementation constraints, and terminology. Disagreement is welcome. Treating another person's access needs or lived experience as a debate topic is not.

This document may evolve as the community and project grow.
